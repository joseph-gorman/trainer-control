How to Connect Your Trainer & HR Monitor

1. Browser Prerequisites

Web Bluetooth requires a Chromium-based browser: Google Chrome, Brave, Edge, or Opera on Mac, Windows, Linux, Chromebook, or Android. (iOS Safari does not support Web Bluetooth; use the Bluefy browser on iPhone/iPad if necessary).

2. Disconnect from Other Apps (Crucial!)

Smart trainers and chest straps can usually connect to only one Bluetooth device at a time. Make sure Zwift, MyWhoosh, Garmin, or phone companion apps are closed completely before clicking connect.

3. Wake Up the Sensors
  • Decathlon/Van Rysel Trainer: Plug it in and spin the pedals a few rotations so the Bluetooth LED blinks/awakes.
  • Heart Rate Strap: Moisten the electrode pads with water or spit and wear it snug against your chest so it turns on and broadcasts.
4. How this App Controls Resistance

When you click 'Connect Trainer', the app claims GATT control via FTMS (0x00) and writes OpCode 0x04 (Set Target Resistance Level) in 0.1% resolution. This behaves exactly like an electronic brake dial—zero shifting required!
